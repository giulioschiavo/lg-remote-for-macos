## English

- After Turn On, the app allows time for the TV to start and retries the connection at 5-second intervals for about 40 seconds. Retries stop on success or certificate/authorization errors, without continuous network discovery.
- A startup status explains the wait; controls and TV data become available after connecting.
- The header shows the TV name while connected and the app name while disconnected. TV names are retrieved when available and can be edited in Settings.
- Hiding the panel from Settings returns to the main remote on reopening.
- Remote icon replaces the Settings close icon to return to controls.
- Updated bilingual screenshots and documentation.

The user confirmed a successful wake after overnight standby. The new reconnection sequence passed simulated tests; validation during a slow real-TV startup remains pending. Universal TV compatibility is not guaranteed.
